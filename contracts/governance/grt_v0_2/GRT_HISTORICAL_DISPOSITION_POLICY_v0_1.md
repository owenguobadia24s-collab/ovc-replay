# GRT Historical Disposition Policy v0.1

Historical and quarantined material remains evidence, not current authority. Every disposition cites exact constitutional rule IDs, evidence references, and QA.

A batch affecting more than five percent of the then-open B0 population requires enhanced independent QA. Batch arithmetic is explicit: the record preserves open-B0 denominator and member count. Disposition never deletes source history, fabricates current ownership, or converts unresolved evidence into current conformance.
