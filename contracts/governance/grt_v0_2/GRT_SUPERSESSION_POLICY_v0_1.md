# GRT Supersession Policy v0.1

**Status:** `PROPOSED_UNADMITTED`  
**Authority effect:** `NONE_PRE_ENFORCEMENT`

Supersession is represented by explicit `SUPERSEDES` relationships and lifecycle generations. Historical artifacts are preserved. A filename containing `FINAL`, `REVISED`, `CURRENT`, or `RATIFIED` has no supersession authority.

Current mandatory dependencies may not target a superseded or historical artifact unless an exact compatibility contract admits that dependency. Corrective work must rebind or explicitly declare compatibility; it must not delete or rewrite historical evidence.
