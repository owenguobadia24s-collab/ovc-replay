# C2 Level and Relation Contract v0.1

Levels are deterministic, first-valid structural references derived only from approved C1 facts. Each level declares a stable type, clock, side, source lineage, confirmation window, lifecycle state, gap behaviour and supersession identity.

Relation sets are complete inventories for an evaluation scope. Every eligible level relation is emitted or excluded with a deterministic reason. Required fields include relation type, side, signed distance, normalized proximity, crossing status, persistence and first-valid time.

Multiple levels may coexist. Hidden best-level selection, outcome ranking, semantic naming and future access are prohibited.
