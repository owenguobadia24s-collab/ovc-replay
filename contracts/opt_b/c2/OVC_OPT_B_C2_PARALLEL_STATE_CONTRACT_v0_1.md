# OVC OPT-B.C2 Parallel-State Contract v0.1

Canonical unit: one C2 state record per eligible C1 record and evaluation scope.

C2 computes five independent axes: LOCATION, MOTION, ORGANISATION, INTERACTION and QUALITY. No axis suppresses another; `overall_state`, `winning_state`, semantic names, episodes, outcomes and trade labels are prohibited.

Inputs are exact ACTIVE_DISCOVERY or ACTIVE_DEVELOPMENT C1 v2 records on GBPUSD, clocks 15M or 2H_A_L, sides BID or ASK. Validation is locked.

Every record binds contract, schema, axis, measurement, scope and parameter-pack versions; exact OPT-A/C1 lineage; first-valid time; active containers; complete relation set; previous contiguous state or null; axis statuses and reason codes.

Chronology is first-valid and outcome-blind. Persistence may cross only contiguous same-release, clock, side, scope and parameter-pack records. Semantic-version changes require full replay; historical records are immutable.

Authority after C2-G1 is fixture/engine implementation only. Market replay, release, selector, activation, probability, exposure, trading and execution remain denied.
