# C2E Boundary Pack Contract v0.2

Authority: inactive `CANDIDATE`/`SHADOW` machinery only. No production pack is selected or activated.

A boundary pack freezes its version/supersession lineage, exact source population/scope, rule set, per-rule dependency roles, onset/effective/confirmation/FVT semantics, priority class 1-8, pair/compound compatibility, SINGLE_OWNER peer ownership, topology constraints, discontinuity behavior, conflict policy, parameter identity, implementation hashes and registry hashes.

Compatibility dispositions are exactly `COMPATIBLE_COMPOUND`, `ORDERED_BY_PRIORITY`, `MUTUALLY_EXCLUSIVE_BY_RULE`, and `INCOMPATIBLE_CONFLICT`. An undeclared pair fails closed. Pairwise compatibility does not prove compound compatibility after higher-priority effects; WP3 must revalidate closure.

Priority classes are frozen: 1 ADMISSIBILITY/QUARANTINE; 2 CAUSAL_DISCONTINUITY; 3 UNRESOLVABLE_CONFLICT; 4 LINEAGE_TOPOLOGY; 5 TERMINATION/COMPLETION; 6 PHASE_MUTATION; 7 CONTINUATION; 8 BIRTH.

Logical pack identity includes all identity-defining rule/dependency/compatibility/priority/parameter/implementation/registry semantics. Rule ordering, parameter-key ordering and non-semantic metadata do not alter identity. Any semantic parameter, precision, compatibility or priority change creates a new logical pack identity. Remap records are comparison evidence only.
