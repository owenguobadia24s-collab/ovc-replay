# C2E Legacy Conformance Contract v0.2

`C2E2-WP4` preserves the historical Market Grammar C2E v0.1 implementation and the blocked Research Operations C2E v0.1 programme as immutable evidence. It does not migrate either in place.

Market Grammar v0.1 is invoked only through a comparison namespace. Its exact historical fixtures, policy, schemas and builder must continue to reproduce their historical IDs/results; its runtime is not selectable by the new C2E2 orchestrator. Historical IDs remain `C2E.EP.*` / `C2E.LD.*` and are never relabelled as v0.2 `C2E.EPISODE.*` identities.

Any cross-version mapping is a `C2ERemapRecord` with `comparison_only=true` and `authority=COMPARISON_ONLY`. A remap is not identity, lineage, causal parentage, canonical SRI key, selector state or proof of semantic equivalence.

The Research Operations programme `OVC-C2E-NEUTRAL-EPISODE-v0.1` remains `BLOCKED` at `C2E-G1`; C2E2 prospectively supersedes only the future implementation route and does not reopen, edit or reinterpret that operator BLOCK.

No historical conformance result grants source-replay, pack-selection, selector, publication, Validation, semantic/family, probability, risk, exposure or execution authority.
