# CEI Descriptive Comparator Contract v0.1

Status: INACTIVE_CONFORMANCE_ONLY. Authority effect: NONE.

The Constraint Evidence Interface (CEI) represents conditional structural regularity only. A CEI claim binds an exact observable `ConstraintCondition`, an exact comparator condition, one frozen denominator-defined `ConstraintPopulationManifest`, one `ConstraintContrastSpec`, typed structural target and reconstructible metric evidence.

## Constitutional rules

1. CEI is outcome-blind OPT-B evidence and does not establish cause, intent, mechanism, prediction, probability, setup quality, risk, exposure or execution meaning.
2. Conditions MUST come from lawful upstream sources and declare `STRUCTURAL`, `TEMPORAL`, `AUXILIARY` or `DATA_QUALITY` type plus one temporal role: `PRE_EXISTING`, `AT_ANCHOR`, `CONCURRENT`, `TIME_VARYING_DURING_TARGET`, `POST_TARGET`.
3. First-valid chronology controls use. A condition not lawfully available by the target anchor MUST NOT be rendered as a precondition. Concurrent and post-target conditions are never preconditions.
4. Comparator B is part of the claim and must be distinct and exact. No post-result comparator selection is allowed.
5. Population is frozen before comparison. Every eligible occurrence reconciles to one explicit population state. Missing, censored, not-comparable and not-evaluable observations never disappear.
6. CEI is method-neutral. `STRATIFIED`, `MATCHED`, `LONGITUDINAL`, `CHRONOLOGICAL` and explicitly declared other designs may be represented, but this packet selects no statistical method, matching threshold or support threshold.
7. Matching narrows a descriptive comparison; it never constitutes causal adjustment.
8. `CEIConstraintAST` is a closed descriptive ontology. Only registry-listed nodes are representable. Cause, causal, mechanism and intent predicates are structurally unavailable. Text lint is secondary defense-in-depth.
9. Constraint targets are `EMERGENCE`, `PERSISTENCE`, `ORGANISATION`, `TRANSITION`, `STRUCTURAL_EVENT`, and `AMBIGUITY_BOUNDARY`.
10. Mechanism hypotheses, if proposed later, are Research Operations objects and cannot mutate CEI evidence.

Rollback is forward supersession of WP8-only artifacts. Historical ESL identities and WP0-WP7 evidence remain immutable.
