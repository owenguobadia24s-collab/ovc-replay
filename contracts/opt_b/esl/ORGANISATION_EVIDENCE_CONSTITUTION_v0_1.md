# OPT-B ESL Organisation Evidence Constitution v0.1

Programme: `OVC-OPTB-ESL-CONFORMANCE-v0.1`  
Packet/Gate: `ESLI-WP7 / ESLI-G7`  
Authority: `INACTIVE_CONFORMANCE_ONLY`; authority delta `NONE`.

## Purpose

WP7 turns exact `SOIViewResult` records into reconstructible organisation-evidence records without selecting a topology, method, representation, family catalogue, threshold or semantic term. A raw view is not support. Every scientific disposition remains unavailable until a separately governed executable `OrganisationDecisionRulePack` exists.

## Record constitution

An `OrganisationMetricRecord` names its exact eligible universe, numerator, denominator, exclusions and missingness. An undefined denominator is `NOT_EVALUABLE`; it is never rendered as zero evidence.

An `OrganisationCorrespondenceEdge` is directional where required and preserves endpoint identities. It may express correspondence, containment, split/refinement, overlap, shared core, contradiction, incompatibility, no correspondence, not-comparable or not-evaluable without aliasing identity.

An `OrganisationDisagreementRecord` identifies the exact disagreement axis: representation, comparison, method, sensitivity, topology, population, chronology or boundary. Disagreement is typed diagnostic evidence and MUST NOT be collapsed into one universal score.

An `InvariantEvidenceRecord` identifies exact membership, relation, component, neighbourhood, ordering or core content that survives declared view perturbations.

An `OrganisationEvidenceSet` is a manifest over exact view, metric, correspondence, disagreement and invariant identities. It records the tested envelope, chronology, nulls and authority. Its WP7 scientific disposition is always `NOT_EVALUATED_RULE_PACK_REQUIRED`.

## Null and envelope rules

`NO_STABLE_FAMILY` is scoped only to the FAMILY topology. It does not mean no hierarchy, overlap, graph, continuum, composition or organisation.

`NO_STABLE_ORGANISATION` is lawful only after all six declared topologies are evaluable over a complete frozen envelope and a separately authorised executable decision rule pack applies. WP7 materialises only an `INTERFACE_ONLY` rule-pack contract, with no thresholds or decision rules.
