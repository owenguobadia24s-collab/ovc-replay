# BCWT B-to-C Origin Contract v0.1

Authority effect: **NONE**. BCWT-R2 is mechanical conformance only.

The default origin is one `FINAL_RETURNED_STRUCTURAL_UPDATE`. Carried bar states and latent states superseded or cleared inside the same update are not origins. Mechanical origins must have null candidate-generation, CandidateOccurrence and CandidateEvaluationAdmission references. `C_ADMITTED_SCIENTIFIC_ORIGIN` is schema-reserved but execution is hard-denied in R2.

First-valid cutoff, segment identity, source-prefix identity, fixed-asset identity, state-row hash, missingness/non-knowledge and break state are identity-bearing. No later outcome may alter origin bytes.
