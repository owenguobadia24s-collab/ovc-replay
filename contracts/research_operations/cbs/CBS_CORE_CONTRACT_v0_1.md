# CBS Core Contract v0.1

Status: bounded implementation constitution under the ratified `C2E-BOUNDARY-STABILITY-0001` design and implementation plan.

CBS measures stability and uncertainty of method-relative temporal structural boundaries. It does not search for or declare one true segmentation. Its outputs are non-probabilistic research evidence and grant no C2E selection, replacement, activation, lifecycle, Validation, publication, risk, trading or execution authority.

## Invariants

The following relations are non-transitive and must remain machine-visible: boundary estimate is not a C2E boundary; method agreement is not independent votes; concordance is not causality; location stability is not episode identity; retrospective break is not first-valid causal break; operator annotation is not ground truth; method failure is not information absence; stable subset is not universal segmentation; benchmark PASS is not C2E activation.

Every identity-bearing CBS object uses UTF-8 canonical JSON with keys sorted, compact separators, no NaN/infinity, and SHA-256 over the payload excluding its declared identity field. Scientific records are append-only or forward-superseded; historical negative, missing, censored, invalid and exposed results are preserved.

`REFERENCE_BOUNDARY_AUDIT` and `CONSENSUS_BOUNDARY_DISCOVERY` have distinct IDs, candidate regions, denominators and claim surfaces. No output may cross estimands. Family/dependence-factorised evidence is non-scalar: voting, averaging, best-configuration selection and duplicate-grid inflation are forbidden.

The minimum core comparator envelope is B0, B1, B2, B3 and B9. B4-B7 are conditional and absent implementations remain `NOT_EVALUABLE`; B8 is reference-only. A broad result is `NOT_EVALUABLE` unless owner-defined reference, online-causal, confirmation-delayed, retrospective and control temporal classes are represented.
