# CBS Correspondence and Two-Estimand Contract v0.1

CBS compares boundary neighbourhoods under a prospectively frozen tolerance grid. Exact timestamp equality is not presumed. Directional deterministic one-to-one correspondence is applied first and preserves missed/extra/unmatched estimates. Split, merge and ambiguous mappings are separate records; gaps and censoring are not disagreement. Comparator identities are immutable through matching.

`REFERENCE_BOUNDARY_AUDIT` conditions on the frozen B0 reference boundary projection. Its regions, denominator and unmatched set cannot establish symmetric discovery. `CONSENSUS_BOUNDARY_DISCOVERY` forms symmetric cross-method candidate regions without privileged ground truth; C2E is one dependent method family. Each estimand has its own identity and opportunity population.

Tolerance, method, threshold, parameter, representation, context and null multiplicity are fully ledgered. Semantically equivalent configurations share a family identity and cannot increase family support. Selecting a favourable tolerance or configuration after observing results is `INVALID_GENERATION`; coercing disagreement into consensus is a hard failure.
