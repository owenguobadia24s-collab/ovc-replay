# CBS Preregistration Freeze and Amendment Policy v0.1

No CBS preregistration is frozen during WP1. A later `CBSPreregistrationFreezeReceipt` must atomically bind source population, owner authorities, B0 projection, all comparator projections/support/configurations, evaluation universe, both estimands, tolerance/correspondence rules, family/dependence factorisation, null/control implementation, endpoint/materiality, complete opportunity/exposure ledgers, capacity/retention, replication reservation and exact independent-review frontier.

After freeze, a byte/mechanical correction is `MECHANICAL_NON_MEANING` only when semantic identity is proved unchanged. Any meaning-bearing change creates `MEANING_BEARING_SUCCESSOR`; the frozen generation is never edited in place. Post-result changes to tolerance, projection, parameter, null, endpoint, materiality, context, replication population or decision rule invalidate the current claim surface.

Rollback is forward-only. Disable or supersede affected generations while preserving source bindings, exposures, failures, negative evidence, external hashes and decision records. `CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` never authorises sampling, interval shortening, method dropping, parameter/tolerance shrinking or representation substitution.
