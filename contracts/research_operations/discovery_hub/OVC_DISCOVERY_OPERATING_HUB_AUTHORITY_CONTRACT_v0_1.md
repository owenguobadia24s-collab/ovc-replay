# OVC Discovery Operating Hub Authority Contract v0.1

Status: `FROZEN_BY_DO_00_V0_3_REALIGNMENT_PENDING_DO_G0_REVIEW`

## 1. Purpose

The Discovery Operating Hub is the governed operating programme that uses the Research Operations Foundation and approved OPT-A/C1/C2 authorities to conduct prospective research, preserve counterevidence and accumulate neutral discovery-friction records.

It is not another market Option. It creates no selector, threshold, semantic, probability, exposure, trading or execution authority.

## 2. Console alignment decision

The operative presentation target is **OVC Research Console v0.3 — Unified Operations Workspaces**.

The Discovery Operating Hub must use the v0.3 three-workspace information architecture:

- `OVERVIEW` — programme awareness, ambient health, release/gate/object/session summaries and attention conditions;
- `RESEARCH` — selected context, brief, cutoff-safe replay, evidence, sessions and queue;
- `SYSTEM` — objects, catalogue, releases, QA/gates, configuration, governance and source-derived activity.

The v0.2 fourteen-route information architecture is superseded as primary navigation. Its authority, health-truth, empty-state, cutoff, determinism, local-only and no-mutation safety contracts remain inherited unless explicitly superseded by a stricter v0.3 contract.

The v0.3 shell, icon rail, workspace switcher, contextual detail drawer, command palette, persistent activity stream and session-state model are owned by the Research Console programme. Discovery Operating Hub work supplies typed programme projections and research-operation content; it must not create a competing shell or independent navigation system.

## 3. Programme dependency

DO implementation beyond design records requires:

1. `RC-A0` amendment records freezing the workspace/panel, session-state, drawer/search and activity contracts;
2. `RC-G0A` PASS accepting those v0.3 contracts;
3. the relevant RC work package to have materialised the target surface before live DO projections are activated.

DO-00 itself does not satisfy or bypass an RC gate.

## 4. Permitted authority after DO-G0

Subject to a separate DO-G0 PASS, the programme may:

- read approved OPT-A, C1 and later C2 releases through registered read-model projections;
- inspect immutable Research Operations records and artifact-catalogue entries;
- project programme status into Overview, research context into Research and governance evidence into System;
- operate cutoff-safe prospective views and explicitly separated review views;
- preserve descriptive context, claim-relative evidence, contradictions, nulls, anomalies, censoring and incidents;
- register neutral recurrence candidates and research-friction records without semantic naming or promotion authority;
- produce review packets and architecture proposals for later operator decisions.

Research-record creation remains outside the v0.3 UI. Until a separate append-only action gate passes, records are created only through the already approved Research Operations CLI/service and are rendered read-only in the console.

## 5. Prohibited authority

The programme must not:

- write directly to repository paths from the console;
- mutate OPT-A, C1, C2 or historical records;
- change selectors, thresholds, parameter packs, release state or publication state;
- consume Validation for discovery, design or threshold selection;
- convert retrospective observations into SUPPORT or CONTRADICTION without a pre-frozen claim;
- name or activate deterministic C2.5 terms from neutral recurrence candidates;
- activate C2E, C2.5, C3, OPT-C, OPT-D or any E-H object;
- create probability, exposure, risk, trading or execution objects;
- add arbitrary filesystem search, repository scans during reruns or unrestricted command execution;
- treat Streamlit session state, drawer selection, filters or scroll position as source authority.

Unregistered actions are prohibited.

## 6. Role and time boundary

- Discovery: `2021-01-01` through `2023-12-31`, approved source role after the applicable operating gate.
- Development: `2024-01-01` through `2024-12-31`, candidate-gated review only.
- Validation: `2025-01-01` through `2025-12-31`, `LOCKED_UNCONSUMED`.

The interface must persistently display role, release identity, source commit, read-model hash, represented time, replay mode and admissible cutoff. Invalid combinations fail closed and never silently fall back.

## 7. Evidence and health discipline

Observation and claim tracks remain separate. A descriptive observation may be `CONTEXT`, `CANDIDATE`, `ANOMALY`, `BOUNDARY`, `NULL` or `CENSORED`. `SUPPORT` and `CONTRADICTION` require an eligible pre-frozen ClaimRecord with discriminator and falsifier.

Every visible interpretation, health cell, evidence badge, queue item and activity event must resolve to immutable source IDs, release lineage, first-valid time, code/configuration versions and QA state.

Health remains multidimensional. No signal is not PASS. Missing, stale, unavailable and not-evaluated references remain visible with consequence and next valid action.

## 8. Context preservation and inspection

Workspace switching, drawer opening, search selection and activity focusing may change presentation context only. They must preserve lawful instrument, release, clock, side, timestamp, cutoff, mode and filters where compatible.

Search is limited to approved indexed fields. The contextual drawer opens in place and exposes source IDs, hashes, lineage, consequence and next valid action. The activity stream is source-derived and excludes ordinary UI view events from authoritative chronology.

## 9. One-way authority and storage

The lawful dependency direction is:

`OPT-A -> C1 -> C2 -> later evidence-gated layers`

Research Operations and the Discovery Operating Hub may inspect and evaluate those records but may not rewrite them. QA may PASS, WARN, BLOCK or QUARANTINE and may not silently repair market or semantic outputs.

Git stores compact contracts, schemas, registries, records, manifests, decisions, tests and summaries. Raw market data, full model streams, recordings, screenshots and bulky evidence remain external and checksum-addressed.
