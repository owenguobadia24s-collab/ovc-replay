# OVC Discovery Operating Hub Authority Contract v0.1

Status: `FROZEN_BY_DO_00_PENDING_DO_G0_REVIEW`

## 1. Purpose

The Discovery Operating Hub is the governed operating programme that uses the completed Research Operations Foundation and the active OPT-A/C1 discovery chain to conduct prospective research, preserve counterevidence and accumulate neutral discovery-friction records.

It is not another market Option and it does not create market, semantic, probability, exposure, trading or execution authority.

## 2. Console alignment decision

The operative interface baseline is **OVC Research Console v0.2** and its fourteen-route registry.

The Discovery Operating Hub must integrate through the existing v0.2 routes, cards, status vocabulary, empty-state rules and action registry. It may not replace them with the proposed Research Console v0.3 three-workspace architecture during this programme.

Research Console v0.3 is recorded as `DEFERRED_DESIGN_REFERENCE_ONLY`. No v0.3 workspace registry, route migration, drawer, command-palette, activity-stream or session-state amendment receives implementation authority from DO-00.

## 3. Permitted authority after DO-G0

Subject to a separate DO-G0 PASS, the programme may:

- read approved OPT-A, C1 and later C2 releases through registered read-model projections;
- inspect immutable Research Operations records and artifact catalogue entries;
- operate cutoff-safe prospective and explicit review views;
- create research records only through the already approved append-only Research Operations service;
- preserve descriptive context, claim-relative evidence, contradictions, nulls, anomalies, censoring and incidents;
- register neutral recurrence candidates and research-friction records without semantic naming or promotion authority;
- produce review packets and architecture proposals for later operator decisions.

## 4. Prohibited authority

The programme must not:

- write directly to repository paths from the console;
- mutate OPT-A, C1, C2 or historical records;
- change selectors, thresholds, parameter packs, release state or publication state;
- consume Validation for discovery, design or threshold selection;
- convert retrospective observations into SUPPORT or CONTRADICTION without a pre-frozen claim;
- name or activate deterministic C2.5 terms from neutral recurrence candidates;
- activate C2E, C2.5, C3, OPT-C, OPT-D or any E-H object;
- create probability, exposure, risk, trading or execution objects;
- implement Research Console v0.3 under this authority.

Unregistered actions are prohibited.

## 5. Role and time boundary

- Discovery: `2021-01-01` through `2023-12-31`, approved for discovery research.
- Development: `2024-01-01` through `2024-12-31`, available only through a separately authorised development-review packet.
- Validation: `2025-01-01` through `2025-12-31`, `LOCKED_UNCONSUMED`.

The interface must display role, release identity, source commit, admissible cutoff and represented time. Invalid combinations fail closed and never fall back silently.

## 6. Evidence discipline

Observation and claim tracks remain separate. A descriptive observation may be `CONTEXT`, `CANDIDATE`, `ANOMALY`, `BOUNDARY`, `NULL` or `CENSORED`. `SUPPORT` and `CONTRADICTION` require an eligible pre-frozen ClaimRecord with discriminator and falsifier.

Every visible statement must resolve to immutable source IDs, release lineage, first-valid time, code/configuration versions and QA state. Missing, stale or unavailable references remain visible.

## 7. One-way authority

The lawful dependency direction is:

`OPT-A -> C1 -> C2 -> later evidence-gated layers`

Research Operations and the Discovery Operating Hub may inspect and evaluate those records but may not rewrite them. QA may PASS, WARN, BLOCK or QUARANTINE and may not silently repair market or semantic outputs.

## 8. Storage boundary

Git stores compact contracts, schemas, registries, records, manifests, decisions, tests and summaries. Raw market data, full model streams, recordings, screenshots and bulky evidence remain external and checksum-addressed.

## 9. DO-00 authority delta

`DESIGN_RECORDS_ONLY`

DO-00 freezes the programme boundary, v0.2 route alignment, authority matrix, deferred-capability register and preflight packet. It activates no operating capability. The next authority-changing decision is `DO-G0 — Operational readiness`.
