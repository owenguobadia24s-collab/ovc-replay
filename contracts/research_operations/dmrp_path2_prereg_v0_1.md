# DMRP Path-2 Native Preregistration Contract v0.1

## Purpose

This additive contract materialises native Research Operations record families for the seven frozen external-theory Path-2 preparations without modifying the frozen Research Operations v0.1 or DMRP v0.2 contracts.

## Record families

`THEORY_RECORD` preserves the exact UNTESTED theory generation and its observable/falsifier/research-conduct constitution. `RESEARCH_PROTOCOL` binds one frozen protocol generation to one exact frozen method/parameter pack. `EXPERIMENT_RECORD` represents the preregistration generation and remains ineffective until every exact source, data-release, code, config, sample, exclusion, QA and dependency binding required by the frozen protocol exists.

## Non-transitivity and authority

Native materialisation does not grant real-source Path-2 execution, durable real-source append, theory support, P2-6 candidate formation, candidate freeze, OPT-C admission, Validation, publication, probability, risk, exposure, trading or execution authority. `authority_effect` is always `NONE`.

`PREREGISTERED_EFFECTIVE` is lawful only when the exact experiment bindings are complete and `dependency_state=BOUND`. Result artifacts are forbidden in this packet. Missing dependencies remain typed and fail closed.

## Current partition

RP-0001..RP-0005 may become effective only after their exact dependencies and experiment bindings are materialised. RP-0003 additionally requires its lawful horizon-output binding. RP-0006 remains dependency unavailable until a second owner-applicable C2E boundary variant exists. RP-0007 Stage A remains population-pending on EC1-RV/Q09; Stage B is excluded and requires a separately versioned material change plus its own authority.

## Identity

Scientific identity is the canonical hash of `record_type + scientific_payload`. Physical attempt IDs and artifact locations are non-scientific envelope fields. Any scientific payload change creates a new semantic identity; records are never rewritten to fabricate continuity.

## Path-1 firewall

Every external Path-2 theory remains `UNTESTED`. Path-2 may not seed, filter, rank, repair or promote EC1 Path-1. Validation remains `LOCKED_UNCONSUMED`.
