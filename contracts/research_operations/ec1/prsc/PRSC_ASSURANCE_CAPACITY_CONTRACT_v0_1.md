# PRSC Assurance & Capacity Contract v0.1

## Scope
PRSCI-WP8 proves synthetic/adversarial mechanical conformance, reference/optimized equivalence and bounded capacity behavior. It grants no real-source PRSC, preregistration, CandidateFreeze, Development/Validation, publication or exposure authority.

## Fixture completeness
All registered adversarial fixtures across dependence, reference, representation, temporal/context, boundary, multiplicity, replication and integrated algebra MUST be executed or explicitly reported NOT_EVALUABLE with a blocking reason. Silent fixture omission is forbidden.

## Reference oracle
Every optimized decision-bearing family MUST be compared against its deterministic reference implementation on identical canonical inputs. Any logical mismatch quarantines the optimized path; the reference path remains the oracle.

## Capacity measurement
Synthetic tiers measure candidate-family scaling, surrogate cost, representation/time/context growth, boundary matching, artifact growth, peak memory and review volume. Measurements are non-scientific operational evidence only.

## Budget freeze
`PRSCOperationalBudget` and `PRSCReviewBudget` are frozen only from measured synthetic/non-scientific evidence. No scientific threshold, population restriction, hidden sampling, top-N review or scope reduction may be introduced to satisfy a budget.

## Fail closed
Capacity exhaustion returns `CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` or `REVIEW_CAPACITY_EXCEEDED`, preserves the registered scientific scope and retains partial artifacts as non-authoritative evidence. Restart/resume MUST reproduce clean-run logical results.

## Output
A conformant run emits a `PRSCMechanicalConformanceBundle` suitable for independent `PRSCI-G8-ALG` review. WP8 itself does not approve that gate.
