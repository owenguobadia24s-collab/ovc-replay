# OVC LSIAC Accession Adjudication Protocol Contract v0.1

**Contract ID:** `OVC-LSIAC-ACCESSION-ADJUDICATION-PROTOCOL-CONTRACT-0.1`  
**Programme:** `OVC-LSIAC-v0.1`  
**Generation:** `OVC-LSIAC-ACCESSION-GEN-0001`  
**Governing constitution:** `OVC-LSIAC-CONSTITUTION-0.1-REVISED-1`  
**Authority effect:** `NONE_PROTOCOL_FREEZE_ONLY`

## Purpose

This contract freezes the decision method for Laboratory Scientific Inheritance and Accession after the exact accession source frontier is frozen and before any substantive survival decision is made. It is a selection-control contract, not a scientific-promotion route, market ontology, owner replacement, semantic admission, Validation gate, publication authority, or execution authority.

## Frozen inputs

The protocol SHALL bind the operator-approved `AccessionFrontierFreezeReceipt` for `OVC-LSIAC-ACCESSION-GEN-0001`, the source-universe manifest, source-passport set, post-v0.5 delta and frozen accession cutoff. No source created at or before the frozen cutoff may be silently excluded. A later-discovered pre-cutoff source is a `FRONTIER_INTEGRITY_INCIDENT`.

## Mandatory two-pass order

Pass 1 classifies source standing, already-source-supported scientific disposition without promotion, exposure, source relation, durability/reproducibility, dependence, triage and blockers for every accession subject. Pass 1 SHALL NOT make final inheritance-role, lifecycle `RETAIN_FORWARD`, destination or architecture-effect decisions.

Pass 2 may begin only after Pass 1 has a frozen completion receipt. Pass 2 applies role admissibility, the total claim-strength cap, scope intersection, dependence and counterevidence completeness before survival/role/destination/effect adjudication.

## Closed vocabularies

All machine records SHALL use the closed enum sets frozen in the protocol and the bound JSON schemas. Invalid values fail closed. `NONE` is a singleton inheritance role and is mutually exclusive with every other role.

## Claim-strength caps

The positive claim cap SHALL be total across all 36 `source_standing × exposure_state` combinations. Any unlisted or invalid combination is `NOT_EVALUABLE`. `SOURCE_DERIVED` is historical context only until exact load-bearing evidence is separately recovered. `PENDING_SOURCE_BINDING` and unresolved `SOURCE_CONFLICT` are not evaluable. `LINEAGE_ALIAS` contributes no independent evidence mass. Fresh confirmation additionally requires valid owner/DMRP standing and the exact applicable protocol/decision.

Typed negative knowledge is separately governed. `NEGATIVE_SCOPED` requires exact or governed externally bound load-bearing evidence, `NEGATIVE_SUPPORTED`, an exact tested universe and explicit `what_is_not_excluded`.

## Source durability

A decision-bearing inheritance object requires 100% retrievable or lawfully regenerable coverage of every load-bearing source. `VERIFIED_ONCE_UNBOUND` alone is not sufficient load-bearing retrievability. Missing or unretrievable load-bearing evidence is `SOURCE_BLOCKED`.

## Dependence and aggregation

Aliases, repeated rounds, overlapping populations and sibling representations SHALL NOT become evidence mass by record count. The dependence graph is mandatory. Multi-source support defaults to scope intersection. Scope union requires an explicit transport/replication synthesis. Material counterevidence remains linked and visible.

## Review

Every reviewer SHALL declare authorship relationship, source exposure, downstream-outcome exposure and conflicts. Independent review is mandatory for `CANONICAL_PRINCIPLE`, `CANONICAL_CAPABILITY`, `CROSS_ARCHITECTURE_AMENDMENT_REQUIRED`, and source-conflict resolution. `OBSERVER_FACULTY` is not silently added as a new constitutional high-impact class; if it creates a cross-architecture amendment, that existing trigger applies.

## Destination and ontology firewall

A retained object may have one controlling owner/authority destination where applicable and zero or more read-only/design consumers. No owner is invented. Register identity, title, role, recurrence or implementation does not create ESL/C3/C2.5/C2E ontology or semantics.

## Protocol mutation

The protocol is immutable for generation `OVC-LSIAC-ACCESSION-GEN-0001` once its freeze receipt is materialised. A material change after survival outcomes are visible requires a successor accession generation or an explicitly exploratory reinspection. Administrative correction is lawful only when the protocol canonical hash and decision semantics do not change.

## Rollback

Rollback is forward-only. Source evidence, protocol bytes, prior decisions and review evidence are never destructively rewritten or deleted.
