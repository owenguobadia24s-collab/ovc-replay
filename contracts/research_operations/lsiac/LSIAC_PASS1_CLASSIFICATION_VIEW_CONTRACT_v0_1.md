## Deterministic source classification

The view reconstructs all source passports from the frozen codebooks and fails closed unless all 434 passports and exactly 432 unique subjects are present. Per subject, source standing, availability and reproducibility are aggregated conservatively to the weakest standing in the subject's bound passports. `PENDING_SOURCE_BINDING` forces `NOT_EVALUABLE`; `SOURCE_DERIVED` remains historical/source context and records exact-source debt for any stronger later claim.

Baseline scientific disposition and exposure are not inferred from titles, phase status, recurrence, current architecture or the initial inheritance docket. They default to `UNRESOLVED` / `UNKNOWN` unless a source-exact post-v0.5 delta record already contains the exact scoped disposition/exposure. This is Pass-1 source-state preservation, not a claim downgrade or promotion.

## Dependence view

Pass 1 may assert `SHARED_SOURCE_DEPENDENT` only where distinct subjects have the same frozen normalized source locator. The absence of an edge means **nothing about independence**. No `INDEPENDENT_BY_FROZEN_CRITERION` edge is generated by this algorithm.

## Pass-1 forbidden outputs

The view SHALL contain no final inheritance role, lifecycle `RETAIN_FORWARD`, destination binding, architecture effect, surviving statement, scientific promotion or authority expansion. Any such field invalidates Pass-1 closeout.

## Freeze and replay

The Pass-1 freeze receipt binds the functional view identity, exact algorithm blob, frozen source identities, exact subject/passport counts, and required conformance tests. Pass 2 may consume only this exact view or a materialized replay proven equivalent to it. A material algorithm change requires a successor Pass-1 view generation and a new freeze receipt; it may not silently alter an already-consumed Pass-1 classification.

## Rollback

Forward-only. Preserve the frozen source frontier, protocol, Pass-1 algorithm identity, view identity, tests and any later materialized replay. No source or historical classification evidence is destructively rewritten.
