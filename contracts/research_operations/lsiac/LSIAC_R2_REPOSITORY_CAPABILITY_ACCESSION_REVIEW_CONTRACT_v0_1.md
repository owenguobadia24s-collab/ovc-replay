**Authority effect:** `NONE_REVIEW_METHOD_ONLY`

## Purpose

LSIAC-R2 is a successor, capability-first review of the difference between the scientific machinery that is repository-effective now and the durable scientific machinery, information planes, controls and negative knowledge developed in the Laboratory outside the repository.

The review is not a second census of every experiment. It exists to decide what persistent machinery is worth transporting repo-side so that OVC can execute governed research over longer populations, additional separately authorised asset-class populations and multiple separately authorised clocks without rebuilding specialised research logic by hand.

The operational objective is therefore:

```text
repo execution substrate
+ reusable scientific faculties
+ portable specialised ResearchProtocols/adapters
= scalable research without turning every experiment into permanent architecture
```

## Scope correction from GEN0001/GEN0002

GEN0001/GEN0002 remain immutable historical accession generations. LSIAC-R2 does not rewrite their source census, adjudication, decisions or closeout.

R2 applies a different membership question:

> Is this object a durable repository capability, observer faculty, retained information plane, methodological doctrine, negative constraint, reference oracle or owner handoff requirement whose value survives the research programme that produced it?

An ordinary research programme, hypothesis, study nomination, one-off population result or specialised experiment is not an accession candidate merely because it exists or was scientifically useful.

## Operator motive frozen by R2

Repository-side machinery is justified primarily when it enables one or more of:

1. execution over substantially longer governed populations;
2. reuse across separately authorised instruments or asset classes;
3. reuse across separately authorised clocks and multiclock comparisons;
4. deterministic replay, checkpoint/restart, source lineage and denominator reconciliation;
5. transport of specialised research through typed ResearchProtocols or stage adapters without promoting the specialised programme itself into permanent architecture.

This motive does **not** grant a new instrument, market, provider, side or clock. Those remain separately owner/operator governed.

## Existing execution substrate first

R2 SHALL test for reusable repository infrastructure before proposing new infrastructure. In particular, IROF's existing `PopulationSpec + PipelineProfile + StageSpec/adapters + AuthorityBindings` execution model is treated as the default generic transport/execution plane when current repository evidence confirms it remains available.

A new generic runner, scheduler, checkpoint system, cache system or research-transport framework is prohibited unless a concrete gap remains after IROF/Research Operations reuse is assessed.

## Eligibility classes

### Eligible for accession review

- `CANONICAL_CAPABILITY`: reusable deterministic scientific machinery whose semantics are stable enough to implement as a governed capability.
- `OBSERVER_FACULTY`: reusable observer-side transformation/state faculty that changes what OVC can represent or inspect without claiming predictive or semantic authority.
- `RETAINED_INFORMATION_PLANE`: information that must remain available to downstream research even when it is not a recurring class or ontology.
- `REFERENCE_ORACLE`: exact historical/replay implementation retained to prove equivalence or diagnose drift.
- `METHODOLOGICAL_DOCTRINE`: reusable research-control rule whose value extends beyond one experiment.
- `NEGATIVE_KNOWLEDGE`: source-supported prohibition or claim cap that prevents the repository from assuming an unearned ontology or relation.
- `HANDOFF_REQUIREMENT`: a missing owner-controlled source/read contract that blocks reusable scientific machinery but must be created by the owning programme rather than by LSIAC.

### Not accession candidates by default

- ordinary ResearchProtocols or experiments;
- future research nominations;
- one-off result packs or charts;
- population-specific hypotheses and parameter searches;
- specialised diagnostics that can be transported as a ResearchProtocol/StageSpec when needed;
- proposed architectures that have not completed their own design/review/ratification route;
- semantic labels, family labels, causal boundary claims, predictive claims or exposure claims not separately authorised by their owners.

## Review questions

Every candidate SHALL answer all of the following.

1. **Repository equivalence:** Is equivalent machinery already implemented or available in current main?
2. **Durability:** Does the capability remain useful outside the exact experiment/population that produced it?
3. **Scale/transport value:** Does repo-side implementation materially improve long-population, cross-population, cross-asset or multiclock research execution?
4. **Source standing:** Are the load-bearing algorithm/evidence sources exact, retrievable or lawfully regenerable enough to support the proposed inheritance role?
5. **Owner boundary:** Which programme owns the source truth? Can LSIAC implement only a consumer capability, or is an upstream owner handoff required first?
6. **Non-transitivity:** What does implementation explicitly *not* promote or activate?
7. **Minimality:** What is the smallest reusable surface worth retaining permanently?

## RRSCG decomposition rule

RRSCG SHALL NOT be considered as one indivisible accession object. At minimum, R2 SHALL distinguish:

- the R2 representation-robust continuation-constraint compiler/kernel;
- D9 observer-state / constraint-geometry / kinematics machinery;
- the D10 reducer as a narrow successor at the reducer layer only;
- specialist memory, region, driver, pretransition, boundary and SFF studies as transportable research unless separately shown to be durable core machinery;
- multiclock correspondence/alignment machinery from claims about a shared categorical state lattice.

D10 supersession SHALL NOT be interpreted as superseding the full D9 state/dynamics machinery unless exact source evidence says so.

## Multiclock rule

Clock is treated as an observer coordinate. R2 may recommend reusable clock-indexed alignment, nesting, correspondence and comparison machinery. It SHALL NOT infer one shared categorical phase/state lattice from clock covariance or morphology resemblance.

`TV120_NATIVE` SHALL NOT be silently equated with canonical `2H_A_L`.

## Specialised research transport rule

Where a research line can be expressed as a frozen ResearchProtocol plus typed StageSpec/adapter over already-lawful source surfaces, R2 SHALL prefer `PORTABLE_SPECIALISED_RESEARCH` over permanent architectural accession.

Examples include representation-robustness studies, memory/metastability challenges, pretransition studies, boundary correspondence studies and future OPGS evaluations unless they later yield independently durable machinery.

## Authority and non-grants

This contract authorises no C2 owner-source change, no new provider/instrument/market/side/clock, no selector activation/replacement, no `ACTIVE_DISCOVERY`, `ACTIVE_DEVELOPMENT` or `ACTIVE_VALIDATION`, no semantic/family/candidate/theory promotion, no C2E boundary change, no C2P/C2.5/C3 activation, no canonical/R2 publication, no SFF probability, no Validation consumption and no probability/risk/exposure/trading/execution/agent-write authority.

## Stop condition

R2 SHALL stop at the first owner-reserved handoff or accession authority boundary after producing a source-bound repository-gap matrix and the smallest lawful successor route. Missing current C2 owner read authority is not repaired by LSIAC, historical reference code, private-engine introspection or chat-side reconstruction.

## Rollback

Forward-only. This R2 review may supersede only the *interpretation* of what the next accession review should examine. GEN0001/GEN0002 evidence and decisions remain immutable and addressable.
