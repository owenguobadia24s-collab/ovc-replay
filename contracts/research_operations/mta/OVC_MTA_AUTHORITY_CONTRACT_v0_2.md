# OVC MTA Authority Contract v0.2

## Authority classes

### READ

May read current contracts, selectors, manifests, accepted June compact records, PR #202 construction evidence, RO4 compact records and explicitly approved external artifacts. Validation, secrets, undeclared roots and mutable provider workspaces are denied.

### DERIVE

May create deterministic summaries, reason-code ledgers, rates, matrices, occurrences, three cluster variants, sensitivity reports, readiness evidence and replaceable read models. May not repair bars, relabel historical C2 records, convert RO4 identities or create semantic outputs.

### WRITE

May write bounded audit court records, schemas, fixtures, tests, QA, checkpoints and programme state to isolated branches. Raw market data, active selectors, parameter packs, release bytes and canonical publication are denied.

### DECIDE

May auto-ratify wholly non-reserved audit gates after PASS QA. May not self-authorise MTA-G0, MTA-A3, MTA-A6 or any MTA-G8 decision.

## One-way dependency

`OPT-A -> C1 -> C2 -> audit evidence -> separately governed C2E/C2.5/C3 proposals`.

Audit findings may evaluate earlier layers but may not rewrite them. Every changed classification uses an immutable registry amendment. Every formula, threshold, reset or clock change requires a separate plan and replay.

## Retained denials

No selector/release mutation, Validation consumption, R2 publication, C2E episode authority, C2.5 event authority, C3 semantics, outcome join, probability, risk, exposure, trading, execution, agent write, direct main write, force-push, deletion or history rewriting.
