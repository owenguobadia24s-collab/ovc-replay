# OVC MTA Readiness Synthesis and Read-Only Route Contract v0.1

**Programme:** `OVC-MTA-v0.2`  
**Packet:** `MTA-WP7`  
**Gate:** `MTA-G7`

## Purpose

Synthesize accepted MTA-WP1 through MTA-WP6 evidence into criterion-level readiness records and local read-only Research Console routes. The synthesis may recommend only whether a separate design or review plan should be prepared. It activates no clock, continuity rule, episode layer, event layer, semantic layer, selector, release or Validation authority.

## Bound routes

Exactly nine logical routes are exposed inside the existing three-workspace console:

`/flow`, `/translation`, `/computability`, `/context`, `/markers`, `/clusters`, `/ro4-comparison`, `/readiness`, `/capacity`.

A fourth workspace is prohibited. Every route is a projection over the immutable WP7 reference. Missing or invalid evidence fails closed.

## Readiness rules

- `MTA-G8-CLOCK` may recommend preparation of a separate clock/continuity review plan only.
- `MTA-G8-C2E` may recommend preparation of a neutral episode-layer plan only.
- `MTA-G8-C2.5` is criterion-level and per-rule. Zero-fire and never-evaluable rules cannot inherit eligibility from other rules.
- `MTA-G8-C3` requires stable lawful inputs; no semantic design recommendation may conceal incomplete quality, parent or independence evidence.
- RO4 remains a separate programme. Absence of RO4-G6 evidence is `NOT_DETERMINABLE`, not agreement.
- Recommendations are evidence beneath the criteria, never authority badges.

## Authority

All presentation is local and read-only. Formula, threshold, clock, continuity, marker meaning, cluster meaning, model, family, candidate, selector, C2E/C2.5/C3 activation, Validation consumption, R2 publication, probability, risk, exposure, execution and research writes remain denied.

## Acceptance

MTA-G7 passes only when all nine routes are present, source identities and accepted findings are retained, all per-rule C2.5 dispositions account exactly, RO4 is kept separate, capacity evidence is explicit, focused and repository-wide tests pass, QA recommends `PASS_WITH_MATERIAL_FINDINGS`, and no reserved authority delta occurs.

## Rollback

Supersede the WP7 reference and route projection with a new immutable version. Preserve all prior evidence, decisions, warnings and hashes.
