# OVC MTA-RO4 Integration Contract v0.1

- RO4 sequence windows/friction records and MTA occurrences/overlap clusters are separate analytical objects with separate IDs, schemas and authority.
- MTA-WP7 may cite RO4 records as supporting or contradicting evidence.
- RO4 evidence is never sufficient alone for C2E, C2.5 or C3 readiness.
- No RO4 sequence candidate may be promoted through MTA.
- No MTA occurrence or cluster may be annotated as an RO4 friction record.
- MTA clustering does not replace RO4 sequence construction or clustering.
- Contradictory conclusions create `CROSS_PROGRAMME_INCONSISTENCY` with exact evidence, scope and consequence.
- A material inconsistency prevents an unqualified recommendation until operator review.
