# P1CDI authority and non-transitivity contract v0.1

Status: mechanically implementable under P1CDII-G0; no authority delta.

P1CDI is a Research Operations inventory/control layer. It may preserve source facts, deterministic inventory state, lineage, evidence vectors, currentness inputs, demand, and read-only projections. It does not own Path-1 scientific dispositions, candidate proposal/freeze/C-admission, capability activation, Validation, publication, probability, risk, exposure, trading, execution, or agent writes.

The following implications are forbidden: DISCOVERED=>GENERAL; RECURRENT=>INDEPENDENTLY_REPLICATED; REPRODUCIBLE=>REPLICATED; DISTINCTION=>CANDIDATE; MECHANICAL_REVIEW_READY=>SCIENTIFIC_ELIGIBILITY; PROPOSAL=>FROZEN; FROZEN=>C_ADMITTED; OPEN_DEMAND=>SCIENTIFIC_PRIORITY; NEXT_DISCOVERY_WORK=>EXECUTION_AUTHORITY; METHOD_GAP=>INFORMATION_GAP; RCCR_NEED_SUPPORTED=>CAPABILITY_ACTIVATION; READ_ONLY_ACTIVE=>CONTINUOUS_INTAKE_AUTHORITY; QA_PASS=>SCIENTIFIC_VALIDITY; P1CDI_ACTIVITY_STATE=>SOURCE_SCIENTIFIC_DISPOSITION; P1CDI_SUMMARY=>OWNER_SCIENTIFIC_ASSESSMENT; RECOMMENDATION_OBJECT=>ACTUATOR.

Missing, ambiguous, or conflicting owner evidence resolves to a typed unresolved/conflict state. Technical success and QA never create authority. Corrections are forward-only; canonical history is not destructively rewritten.
