# P1CDI correspondence executability contract v0.1

Correspondence is multi-plane: semantic, structural core, occurrence/membership, evidence envelope, lineage, and research independence. There is no universal same-structure relation.

The first executable envelope permits only exact semantic equality and relations explicitly asserted by the owning source. Non-exact REFINES, NARROWS, BROADENS, SPLIT, MERGE, and representation-analogue relations are REVIEW_REQUIRED. Retrieval similarity is advisory only.

Split and merge create new lineage while retaining every predecessor. Disappearance is evidence, not deletion. Exact reappearance of unchanged meaning ordinarily adds evidence to the same generation. No correspondence may rewrite owner science or collapse candidate and distinction identity.
