# P1CDI DMRP candidate firewall contract v0.1

Distinction and candidate identities are permanently separate and many-to-many. P1CDI may record ancestry and P1ProposalReadinessAssessment with MECHANICAL_REVIEW_READY, NOT_READY, UNRESOLVED, or BLOCKED. Every P1CDI candidate-link object has `candidate_write: DENIED`. P1CDI may not create Path1CandidateProposal, CandidateFreezePacket, ResearchCandidateGeneration, or CandidateEvaluationAdmission.

FREEZE_CANDIDATE remains an exact source-owner fact and never actuates from P1CDI. Frozen candidate bytes are immutable; later evidence requires successor lineage. Downstream OPT-C/OPT-D/Validation outcomes cannot repair or erase an existing discovery/candidate generation.
