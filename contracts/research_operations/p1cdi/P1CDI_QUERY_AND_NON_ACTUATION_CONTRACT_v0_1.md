# P1CDI query and non-actuation contract v0.1

Decision-bearing queries return the exact source frontier, assessment/profile generation, visibility, currentness, completeness, warnings, and reason trace. Historical AS_OF answers use only evidence first-valid by the cutoff. Silent truncation is forbidden.

NEXT_DISCOVERY_WORK is an advisory, non-scalar recommendation. Its data type and service boundary expose no study creation, task scheduling, RUN/CONTINUE, authority transition, source execution, candidate mutation, or canonical write capability. Any future actuator requires a separately governed service and authority.

Research Console and System Atlas are consumers/deep-link owners. They cannot recompute or mutate P1CDI truth, and P1CDI cannot self-admit a source into either consumer.
