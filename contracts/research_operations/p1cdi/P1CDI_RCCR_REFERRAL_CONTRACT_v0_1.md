# P1CDI RCCR referral contract v0.1

The dependency graph is one-way: source-owned ECx/DMRP/Research Operations evidence -> P1CDI -> RCCR referral -> exact RCCR owner-result reference -> P1CDI route projection.

P1CDI may submit a question, required information, source frontier, and blockers. RCCR owns final gap class, counterfactual sufficiency, and capability-need assessment. RCCR output may route demand but may not become evidence proving or rewriting the distinction that caused the referral. P1CDI may not manufacture capability demand from capability availability.
