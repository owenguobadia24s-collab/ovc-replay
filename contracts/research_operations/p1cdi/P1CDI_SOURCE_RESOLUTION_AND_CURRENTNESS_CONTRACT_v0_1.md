# P1CDI source resolution and currentness contract v0.1

Owner records control their own predicates. ECx/DMRP/Research Operations control source science; DMRP CandidateService controls proposal/freeze/C-admission; RCCR controls gap/capability-need assessment; protected-resource policy controls Validation access. P1CDI controls only its inventory identity, activity, currentness bookkeeping, lineage, and derived read models.

Resolution by newest timestamp, lexicographic path/title, convenient fallback, chat, summary reconstruction, or repository fallback for an externally governed artifact is forbidden. No exposure record yields INDEPENDENCE_UNKNOWN, never independence.

The two-point currentness rule requires two reads of every high-risk owner/source frontier: once before building and once immediately before pointer publication. An unchanged frontier permits publication; movement makes the built projection historical/stale and requires rebuild. Missing or conflicting predicates yield UNRESOLVED_CURRENTNESS or CONFLICT. Currentness remains non-decision-bearing until P1CDII-G2-ALG passes.

WP2 resolver output is advisory fixture evidence until P1CDII-G2-ALG independently reproduces exact source precedence, projection bytes, exact matching, moved-frontier handling, and authority non-transitivity.
