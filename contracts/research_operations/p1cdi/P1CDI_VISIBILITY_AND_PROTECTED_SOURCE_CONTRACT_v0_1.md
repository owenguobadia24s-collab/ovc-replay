# P1CDI visibility and protected-source contract v0.1

Every intake receives a visibility class before indexing or derived aggregation. Incomplete visibility defaults restrictive. Permission intersection applies at object and field level.

Restricted content must not leak through payload fields, titles, identifiers, aggregate counts, graph degree/neighbours, demand text, candidate counts, search suggestions, caches, errors, paths, or metadata. PATH1_SAFE excludes pre-freeze Path-2 candidate-defining predicates, examples, falsifiers, parameter boundaries, and restricted neighbourhoods. Validation is negative-reachability by default and is denied before sensitive resource resolution where the owner contract requires it.

No exposure record is not evidence of independence. Any leak is blocking and incident-bearing.
