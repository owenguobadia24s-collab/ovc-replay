# PMM-DFA Bootstrap Contract v0.1

A conformant bootstrap must preserve these invariants:

- programme identity is `OVC-PMM-0001`;
- bootstrap packet identity is `PMM-DFA-BOOTSTRAP-0001`;
- next gate is `PMM-DFA-R1T-G0`;
- R1T candidates are proposal-only and exactly six in number;
- no proposal acquires semantic, predictive, probability, risk, exposure, trading, execution, publication or Atlas-generation authority;
- candidate freeze is `NONE`;
- Development access is `LOCKED_UNCONSUMED`;
- Validation is `LOCKED_UNCONSUMED`;
- the C0B Atlas remains immutable;
- the 2015 population remains Discovery/exposed evidence and cannot be relabelled as protected Development or Validation;
- Laboratory source bindings are reference/custody bindings, not scientific promotion;
- any future Atlas accession creates a new immutable generation and never rewrites the historical reference standing of earlier observations.

`PMM-DFA-R1T-G0` is an operator-required authority boundary. The bootstrap itself cannot satisfy that gate.
