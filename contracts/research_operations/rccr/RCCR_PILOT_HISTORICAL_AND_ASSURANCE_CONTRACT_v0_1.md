# RCCR Pilot, Historical and Assurance Contract v0.1

Programme: `OVC-RCCR-CONFORMANCE-v0.1`  
Packet: `RCCRI-WP6A`  
Authority effect: `NONE`

## Pilot boundary

The pilot qualifies RCCR mechanics, not EC1 science. The admitted question population is exactly EC1-Q01 through EC1-Q10 from the exact repository-bound question registry and evidence-requirement registry. Real-source EC1 authority remains `NONE`; E1/R1 outcomes are excluded; Validation remains locked and unconsumed. Question definitions and pre-evidentiary review requirements are decision-bearing only for RCCR mechanism qualification. No pilot record may produce an evidentiary EC1 answer or infer a scientific result from missing real-source evidence.

## Question records

Each admitted question preserves exact source identity, source-explicit requirement projection, current owner-state frontier reference and a pre-evidentiary assessment record. The assessment must state that no real-source evidence was consumed, no EC1 scientific answer was produced, and no unsupported `INFORMATION_GAP` was promoted. The lawful next route remains the separately governed real-source EC1 gate and E1/R1 sequence.

## Full assurance population

Every full assurance run executes all AV01-AV24 and G01-G12. Silent sampling or top-N assurance is forbidden. A missing or extra case invalidates the run. G01-G12 identifiers follow the ratified plan exactly: CURRENT_STACK_SUFFICIENT, METHOD_GAP, DENOMINATOR_GAP, DATA_GAP, OWNER_SEMANTICS_GAP, IMPLEMENTATION_GAP, AUTHORITY_GAP, PROTOCOL_EXCLUSION, GENUINE_INFORMATION_GAP, UNRESOLVED_GAP, NEED_SUPPORTED and NEED_CONTRADICTED. Historical WP4 test-ID drift is preserved as historical evidence and corrected prospectively without changing reference algorithm semantics.

## Fixture currentness

Every assurance case is bound to the governing plan/design, RCCR reference/need contracts, EC1 interfaces, DMRP pre-evidence binding and owner-state model. Semantic changes create a currentness trigger. Stale fixtures require a successor generation and may not be silently rewritten. Currentness is checked before the full assurance run and must be checked again before pilot exit.

## Historical counterfactual discipline

Historical cases may use only exact artifacts available at or before the declared decision cutoff. Hindsight inputs, post-cutoff artifacts and selective source admission are forbidden. Missing or ambiguous historical material yields `NOT_AVAILABLE`, never a forced PASS or `INFORMATION_GAP`. The bounded pack includes method-first challenge provenance, a denominator/authority-entangled limitation, an explicit authority limitation, a Capability Without Demand control, and an intentionally unavailable genuine-information-pressure stratum because no exact pre-cutoff source supports it.

## Human review and workaround pressure

Review-load measures are denominator complete and descriptive only. The >30% HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED share is an operational trigger that defaults scale-out recommendation to DEFER; it is not a scientific threshold. Zero routed reviews or zero observed workarounds are measured zeroes, not proof of future zero burden. Decision-bearing external rationale requires exact provenance before use.

## Resource actuals

Planning resource estimates remain estimates rather than schedule SLOs. Human person-day actuals and independent reviewer effort may not be invented when instrumentation is absent; unavailable actuals are recorded explicitly. Independent adversarial reviewer effort remains pending until `RCCRI-G-ADVERSARIAL-REVIEW`.

## Gate boundary

RCCRI-G6A may auto-ratify only mechanical pilot completion with no scale-out. After WP6A integration, `RCCRI-G-ADVERSARIAL-REVIEW` is mandatory and must be decided by the bound independent reviewer outside Research Operations and RCCR implementation before WP7A may begin. No PASS at G6A grants pilot scale-out, capability activation, scientific promotion, publication, probability/risk/exposure/trading/execution or agent-write authority.
