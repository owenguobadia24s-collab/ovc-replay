## Mandatory denials

RO4 cannot mutate, rebuild, publish, supersede or select C2; tune any C2 measurement or threshold; activate Development; consume Validation; create canonical episodes, C2E, C2.5 or C3 authority; merge, rank, annotate or promote Pattern Discovery candidates; expose synthetic ablations to operators; present empirical counts as likelihood; silently sample; join outcomes; create probability, risk, exposure, trading, execution or agent-write authority; write R2; deploy the Console remotely; or write main from runtime code.

## Gate boundary

RO4-G0, RO4-G4, RC-G5 and RO4-G6 are operator-required. RO4-G1, G2, G3 and G5 are auto-ratifiable only when their complete acceptance and QA conditions pass and the proposed authority delta stays wholly non-reserved.

## Rollback
