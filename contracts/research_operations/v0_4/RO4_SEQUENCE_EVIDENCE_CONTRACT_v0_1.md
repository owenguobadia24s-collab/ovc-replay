## Windows

A sequence contains 2–16 contiguous state records and 1–15 exact connecting transitions, one role, one release, one clock and one side. Gaps, quarantine, unavailable parents and chronology breaks terminate membership. Boundary sources are `FIXED_ROLLING`, `TRIGGER_BOUND`, `OPERATOR_ANNOTATED`, `REPLAY_REVIEW` or `LIVE_PROSPECTIVE`.

## Signatures

Exact signatures include ordered five-axis vectors, ordered changed-axis sets, role, clock, side, parameter pack, state registry, policy version, context and parent-change markers. Canonical JSON SHA-256 is the exact identity. Optional near recurrence uses only the frozen distance registry and exposes every component, weight, scale and tie-break rule.

## Neutral recurrence and real controls

Neutral recurrence groups repeated signatures with real matched non-candidates, broader-population controls, boundary shifts, temporal offsets, operation-mode controls, cross-partition real sequences and counterexamples. Semantic labels and promotion paths are prohibited.

## Vocabulary

Use exact state/transition descriptions, `sequence candidate`, `neutral recurrence group`, exact count with denominator and bounded review disposition. Do not emit setup, signal, opportunity, breakout, reversal, continuation, strong, weak, family, archetype, prediction, probability, valid pattern or invalid pattern.

## Signature diversity

Every full recurrence population and operator batch receives a deterministic diversity audit. For at least 100 candidates, warning thresholds are entropy below 0.55, top-1 above 12%, top-5 above 30% or top-10 above 45%. Below 100 candidates is `INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE_FOR_DIVERSITY_AUDIT`, never PASS. No signature exceeds 20% of an operator batch unless impossible and disclosed. Unresolved warning requires operator acknowledgement before RO4-G4.
