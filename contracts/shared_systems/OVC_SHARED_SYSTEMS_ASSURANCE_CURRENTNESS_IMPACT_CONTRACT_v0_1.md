# OVC Shared Systems Assurance, Qualification Currentness and Change Impact Contract v0.1

Status: inactive/reference constitution under `SHSI-WP4`; authority effect `NONE`.

Assurance is claim-scoped. An `AssuranceAssertionResult` proves only its exact
assertion, subject, applicability and evidence requirements. PASS/FAIL/NOT_EVALUABLE
is separate from blocking disposition; mandatory failure cannot be averaged away.
Mechanical conformance is not scientific qualification, authorisation or activation.

`QualificationRecord` is immutable evidence scoped to exact target, release,
generation, capability, role, environment, source, semantic scope and evidence.
`QualificationCurrentness` is a rebuildable projection. Generation, environment,
source or semantic drift yields `STALE`; quarantine and explicit revocation remain
separate typed outcomes.

Semantic change invalidates only the transitive closure of explicit
`ImpactDependencyEdge` objects. Implementation equivalence requires an explicit
evidence reference and invalidates the changed object only; the word "refactor" is
not proof. Ambiguous classification fails conservatively across the declared universe
and records replay obligations and unresolved impacts. Every selective plan carries a
deterministic unaffected-set proof; it never silently guesses an unaffected set.

Incidents and quarantines preserve evidence, impact assessment, and rollback/release
conditions. They are admissibility planes, not deletion, lifecycle termination or authority revocation.
Read models are deterministic, rebuildable and non-authoritative.

Nothing in this contract creates scientific/operator authority, accesses Validation,
changes a current consumer or domain qualification, or publishes canon/R2.
