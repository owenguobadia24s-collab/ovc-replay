## Visual constitution
- Dark near-black/navy analytical surfaces; thin blue-toned borders; restrained elevation.
- High information density at 1920×1080.
- Strong asymmetric hierarchy with one dominant primary analytical surface.
- Compact context navigation; integrated evidence/inspector access.
- Matrix/graph/timeline/AST/table/chart surfaces must look like professional analytical instruments, not HTML demos.
- Tight intentional whitespace; no large unexplained dead zones.
- Stable domain identity: Investigate blue, Research purple, Evidence green/teal, Control amber/orange.
- Low-radius technical geometry; compact tabs/toolbars; no pill-heavy generic SaaS styling.

## Blocking anti-patterns
1. Generic card dashboard.
2. Equal-box semantic gallery.
3. Sparse shell / dead whitespace.
4. Raw HTML table/list/`<pre>` as final primary component.
5. Chart-first global product identity.
6. Consumer trading / predictive / execution aesthetic.
7. Write-affordance leakage.
8. Decoration replacing typed semantics.
9. Evidence detached from selected object.
10. Premature operator gate after an intermediate pass.
