# Development history

The release history is organized by research boundary, not by file creation
time. Each directory contains the compact portion of a historical release:
contracts, reports, manifests, validation records, summaries, and compact
semantic registries. All market tables and generated evidence streams remain
external and are indexed separately.

## OPT-A — trusted market record

- `opt-a-reconciliation-2026-h1`: reconciles M1-derived and H1-derived 2H bars.
- `opt-a-discovery-2026-h1`: sealed H1 2026 discovery authority.
- `opt-a-validation-2025`: sealed 2025 holdout authority.

## OPT-B — deterministic market language

- `opt-b-reference-registry-0.1`: confirmed swings and rolling boundaries.
- `opt-b-complete-replay-0.1`: exhaustive seven-term replay metadata.
- `opt-b-relevance-0.2`: structural retirement and sensitivity review.
- `opt-b-state-0.3`: parallel-axis comparison against persistent v0.2 state.
- `opt-b-state-0.3a`: acceptance relation inventory.
- `opt-b-state-0.3b-review`: accepted-frontier semantic review.
- `opt-b-state-0.3b-ratified`: current ratified descriptive state.

## OPT-C — neutral forward measurement

- `opt-c-anchor-0.1`: event-anchor release contract, manifest, and summary.
- `opt-c-coverage-0.1`: seven-horizon path coverage and censoring.
- `opt-c-measure-0.1.1`: neutral outcome measurement with frontier nullability repair.
- `opt-c-semantic-review-0.1`: arithmetic, nesting, overlap, and support review.

## OPT-D — evidence, validation, and gating

- `opt-d-cohort-0.1`: overlap-clustered cohorts.
- `opt-d-contrast-0.1`: cluster-balanced comparisons.
- `opt-d-story-0.1`: repeated-story contract, report, summary, and archetype registry.
- `opt-d-review-0.1`: frozen hypothesis review.
- `opt-d-validate-0.1`: untouched 2025 validation chain.
- `opt-d-robustness-0.1`: concentration and leave-one-month-out review.
- `paper-playbook-gate-0.1`: final frozen paper-playbook decision.
