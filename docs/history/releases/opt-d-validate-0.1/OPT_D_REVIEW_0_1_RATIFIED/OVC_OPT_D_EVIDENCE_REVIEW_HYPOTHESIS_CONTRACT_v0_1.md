# OVC OPT-D Evidence Review and Hypothesis Contract v0.1

**Contract ID:** `OPT-D-REVIEW-0.1`  
**Parent:** `OPT-D-STORY-0.1`  
**Status:** `BUILT FOR OPERATOR RATIFICATION — NOT PREREGISTERED`

## Purpose

This contract converts the complete H1 repeated-story inventory into an
outcome-neutral review ledger and a pending hypothesis register. It does not
validate any story and grants no probability, edge, recommendation, trade or
execution authority.

## Frozen review admission

Every parent archetype is dispositioned solely by its already-ratified
distinct-overlap-cluster repetition label:

- `REPEATED_DESCRIPTIVE_SUPPORT` becomes
  `CANDIDATE_FOR_BATCH_PREREGISTRATION`;
- `REPEATED_LIMITED_SUPPORT` remains limited-support inventory;
- `REPEATED_MINIMAL_SUPPORT` remains minimal-support inventory;
- `SINGLETON_INVENTORY` remains singleton inventory.

Endpoint alignment, return magnitude, excursion dominance, frontier outcome,
monthly contrast sign and narrative appeal cannot affect admission. Candidate
ratification is a batch decision. Any selective exclusion requires a semantic
invalidity reason fixed without reference to favourable or adverse H1 results.

## Antecedent and response separation

The event-time antecedent contains only:

- closed event clock;
- broad event-family set;
- event direction;
- eligibility after the event anchor closes.

The forward response contains:

- approved observation horizon;
- endpoint alignment;
- favourable/adverse excursion dominance;
- first path extreme;
- continuation state;
- primary-frontier outcome;
- endpoint range third.

Forward-response fields can never determine event eligibility. A validation
match requires all frozen qualitative response fields to match exactly.

## H1 classification

Every candidate is labelled
`IN_SAMPLE_EXPLORATORY_PENDING_OPERATOR_RATIFICATION`. H1 is discovery evidence
only. Candidate count, monthly coverage, representative cases, counterexamples,
exact semantic signatures and B-state lineage remain visible.

## Frozen untouched-validation rules

Validation requires a new sealed OPT-A release with no temporal overlap with
2026 H1 and unchanged OPT-B, OPT-C, OPT-D story and review definitions.

- Evaluable: at least 10 distinct antecedent overlap clusters across at least
  four calendar months.
- Structurally reappeared: at least 10 exact matching story clusters across at
  least four calendar months.
- Otherwise, an evaluable hypothesis is
  `STRUCTURAL_STORY_NOT_REAPPEARED`.
- Insufficient antecedent exposure is
  `NOT_EVALUABLE_INSUFFICIENT_ANTECEDENT_COVERAGE`, not failure.
- Any lineage or definition drift invalidates the run.
- A counter-story shares the frozen antecedent and horizon but has opposite
  endpoint alignment and/or opposite held-versus-lost frontier polarity.
  Contradictory response clusters greater than or equal to matching clusters
  raise a counter-story alert.
- All supplied months, including zero-support months, must be reported.

These are recurrence and coverage rules. They do not establish independence,
conditional probability or edge.

## Censoring and authority

The strict OPT-C coverage and censoring contract remains binding. The 24-hour
horizon is coverage-only and 48 hours remains blocked. Threshold changes,
candidate deletion, metric substitution and story redefinition are prohibited
after holdout data is opened.
