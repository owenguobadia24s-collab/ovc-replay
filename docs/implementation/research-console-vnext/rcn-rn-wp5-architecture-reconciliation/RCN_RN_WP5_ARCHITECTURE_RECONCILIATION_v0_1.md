## Purpose

Reconcile the unstarted `RCN-RN-WP5B` / `RCN-RN-WP5C` implementation topology with the current repository owner architecture without reopening the ratified Research Console product constitution, weakening any scientific firewall, or granting new Research-source presentation authority.

The original WP5 safeguards remain binding: source ownership is preserved; correspondence is not identity; no Path winner meter is permitted; negative/divergent evidence remains visible; frozen candidate identity cannot be repaired downstream; Validation remains locked/separate; the console remains GET/read-only; and any first-new real Research-source presentation remains operator-gated at `RCN-RN-G5-FIRST-NEW-SOURCE`.

## Finding

The original WP5B/WP5C source boundaries remain scientifically sound, but their implementation ownership map is stale relative to current `main`.

Since the governing WP5 PPM was frozen:

1. DMRP has materialised a stronger canonical Research Operations identity and record constitution, including immutable semantic identity, Path 1 / Path 2 research modes, successor-not-rewrite semantics, candidate-generation lineage and explicit authority-free records.
2. ESL and Path-1 distinction work now expose governed bridge/firewall contracts into DMRP; these preserve source identity and prohibit candidate fabrication or cross-mode identity collapse.
3. RCCR now owns source-native Research Coverage Matrix, typed gap queues, Path-1/Path-2 correspondence, independence/exposure state and descriptive Next Research Route projections. The Console must not duplicate this cross-mode interpretation logic.
4. EC1/PRSC now owns a materially richer scientific-challenge layer: representation, temporal/context, dependence/reference, multiplicity, challenge/disposition, preregistration and later real challenge execution. The old WP5C label `OPT-C neutral consequences / OPT-D comparative evidence` is therefore too narrow to describe the current Evaluation surface.
5. System Atlas now owns canonical system search/trace/dependency/impact/explain/authority/ownership/why-blocked/history/diff queries. Research Console must not implement a competing system-governance explorer.

## Reconciled ownership constitution

### DMRP owns

- Path 1 and Path 2 research-mode semantics.
- DMRP studies, evidence-cycle generations and immutable `ResearchCandidateGeneration` identity.
- DMRP candidate-generation lineage and source-owned research dispositions.
- Candidate scientific identity and successor/supersession semantics.

The phrase `one CandidateGeneration constitution` in the original WP5B is retained only for the canonical DMRP `ResearchCandidateGeneration` constitution. It does not collapse all OVC candidate-like identities into one universal candidate type.

### Source programmes own adjacent candidate identities

ESL, P1CDI, C2P and future owner systems retain their own typed proposal, language, structural and object candidate identities. Cross-links into DMRP are evidence/provenance relations only unless the DMRP owner materialises an exact lawful ResearchCandidateGeneration.

### RCCR owns cross-mode research interpretation

RCCR is the owner of:

- Research Coverage Matrix;
- typed gap queues;
- METHOD_FIRST and other source-native gap routing;
- Path-1/Path-2 correspondence;
- independence/exposure state;
- descriptive Next Research Route.

Research Console may render and navigate these owner read models but must not recompute, rank, repair or effectuate them.

### EC1/PRSC owns scientific challenge

PRSC is the owner of challenge evidence and protocol state including representation, temporal/context, dependence/reference, multiplicity, challenge/disposition and preregistration records. Until the exact owner prerequisites for real challenge execution are satisfied, the Console must expose only lawful protocol/readiness/absence state and must not fabricate real results.

### OPT-C / OPT-D own downstream evaluation

Original WP5C OPT-C / OPT-D scope is retained as a downstream evaluation lane. Downstream evidence may not rewrite, repair or erase an earlier candidate or scientific generation.

### System Atlas owns system-governance queries

Where the Research Console needs lineage, authority, ownership, why-blocked, history or impact explanations, it should use/deep-link to canonical Atlas projections when lawfully available. Atlas is not a correctness dependency for WP5 Research presentation unless a later owner contract explicitly makes it one.

## G5 clarification — source-scoped admission

`RCN-RN-G5-FIRST-NEW-SOURCE` remains the operator-required boundary for a first-new real Research-source presentation. This reconciliation clarifies that admission is source-scoped, not transitive.

A PASS/admission for one owner source does not grant presentation authority for another. Each of DMRP, RCCR, PRSC/EC1, OPT-C, OPT-D and future owner sources must carry an exact source-authority preflight/admission receipt before real presentation.

The following are insufficient on their own to satisfy source admission:

- source implementation exists;
- source is locally readable;
- source has scientific authority for its own programme;
- a bridge/adapter exists;
- another Research source already passed G5;
- existing G4 MARKET/C1/C2/C2E Investigate authority.

## Preserved authority boundaries

This packet grants no new:

- Research-source presentation authority;
- scientific, selector, family, candidate, theory or semantic authority;
- C2P/C2.5/C3 real-source presentation authority;
- Validation access;
- publication authority;
- probability, risk, exposure, trading or execution authority;
- agent or governance write authority.

The Console remains read-only. React/UI may render owner facts but may not compute scientific facts, winners, rankings, routes, candidate repairs or authority.
