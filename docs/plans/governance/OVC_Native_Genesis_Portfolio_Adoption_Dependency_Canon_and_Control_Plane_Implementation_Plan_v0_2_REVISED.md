## Mandatory revised safeguards

1. **PGN-G2A census acknowledgement.** `PGN-WP3` cannot construct candidates until the operator acknowledges or adjusts the exact census population, exclusions, surprises, dossier summaries, classes and review groups.
2. **Progressive PGN-G3 review.** Candidates are grouped by programme class or authority domain. Maximum three candidates per group. The operator must acknowledge each group before the next group is disclosed. Group acknowledgement is not adoption.
3. **EDGE_CHALLENGE_PROTOCOL.** Candidate edges have a 48-hour owner challenge window. A challenged edge immediately loses hard/source-explicit status until accepted source-explicit evidence restores it.
4. **Permanent advisory preview.** Admission preview always displays: “This evaluation is non-binding advisory evidence only. It does not satisfy any gate requirement and does not prevent operator review of proposals that fail preview criteria. PGN-G10 enforcement, if activated, may produce different results.” Every invocation logs acknowledgement and `authority_effect=NONE`.
5. **Independent corpus governance.** `PGN-G9A` accepts a versioned sealed corpus before scoring. Negative cases come from at least two distinct programme sources and at least one adversarial case set is mandatory. The enforcement implementation team cannot be the sole curator.
6. **Read-model performance contract.** Current eight-programme full rebuild should complete within 60 seconds on the recorded reference environment. Exceeding the soft bound emits `REBUILD_LATENCY_WARNING`; no check may be skipped. Partition first by `programme_class`, then `constitutional_parent`, followed by cross-partition reconciliation.

## Constitutional rules

Source precedence is: accepted native registrations/operator decisions; accepted authority/gate/publication/retirement decisions; programme-owned machine-readable state; immutable manifests; ratified plans/contracts; merged PR/commit metadata; open PR/branch evidence; chat/screenshots/uncommitted context.

A generated record becomes native only after an operator accepts exact identity, class, purpose, scope, sources, authority envelope, lifecycle, blockers, next action and migration crosswalk. Historical programmes may use an explicit retrospective scope-audit profile; original intent may not be fabricated.

Hard `REQUIRES`, `GOVERNED_BY`, `BLOCKED_BY` and hard `CONSUMES` edges require source-explicit evidence. Informational `PARENT_OF`, `PRODUCES`, `REFERENCES`, `VALIDATED_BY`, `SUPERSEDES` and candidate relations remain authority-neutral. No adapter-inferred or challenged edge can satisfy a hard prerequisite.

## Packet controls

### Native conversion

Each target receives an exact source dossier, candidate class, native candidate, authority envelope, current or retrospective scope audit, migration crosswalk, unresolved-field ledger and lifecycle projection. Final decisions are per programme: `PASS`, `DEFER`, `BLOCK` or `QUARANTINE`.

### Dependency canon

Use the frozen edge vocabulary: `REQUIRES`, `GOVERNED_BY`, `PARENT_OF`, `SUPERSEDES`, `PRODUCES`, `CONSUMES`, `BLOCKED_BY`, `VALIDATED_BY`, `REFERENCES`, `CONFLICTS_WITH`. Publish exact evidence, source kind, direction, hardness and owner notice. Challenges and resolutions are append-only.

### Warning closure

`MIGRATION_UNCERTAINTY` closes only after accepted native adoption and complete crosswalk. Incomplete coverage remains unresolved unless authoritative evidence or explicit not-applicable treatment exists. Hash mismatch blocks. Duplicate authority sources require an accepted precedence/supersession decision. Stale projections are rebuilt from source; source records are never edited to fit projections. Every closure carries before/after health hashes; bulk suppression is prohibited.

### Read-only Control Plane

Required surfaces: portfolio overview, programme detail, dependency graph, health, impact and admission preview. The route exposes no POST/PUT/PATCH/DELETE equivalent, repository credentials, approvals, merges or enforcement writes. Every view displays represented commit, read-model hash, source freshness and authority disclaimer. The route remains disabled and unregistered during build.

`PGN-G8` decisions: `PASS_READ_ONLY_ROUTE`, `LOCAL_SHADOW_ONLY`, `DEFER`, `BLOCK`, `QUARANTINE`.

### Independent corpus and enforcement

Corpus cases cover admitted programmes, bounded non-programme packets, correction/incidents, maintenance ceilings, blocked/non-admitted proposals, PCCR preparation, scope gaming, ambiguous cases, missing authority/source/three-comparison negatives and adversarial bypass attempts. Any post-acceptance mutation creates a new version and invalidates prior metrics.

Shadow metrics include determinism, corpus integrity, adversarial coverage, false admission, false block, unresolved rate, scope-gaming detection, authority leakage and operator-override trace.

`PGN-G10` decisions: `ACTIVATE_FAIL_CLOSED`, `SHADOW_CONTINUE`, `DEFER`, `BLOCK`, `QUARANTINE`. Activation applies only to new proposals and only the exact tested profile.

## Assurance and stop conditions

Required assurance includes schema validation, source precedence, byte-preserved history, identity uniqueness, census-scope denial, maximum-three review groups, graph cycle/type/authority checks, immediate challenge downgrade, evidence-backed warning closure, deterministic read-model hashes, latency measurement, route write-denial, preview disclaimer logs, independently accepted multi-source adversarial corpus, deterministic shadow replay, complete repository tests and `OVC merge readiness` on exact heads.

Stop for missing/conflicting/non-reproducible authority sources; unacknowledged census deltas; authority/lifecycle changes without operator decision; inferred hard edges; unresolved challenges; warning laundering; route write/listener requirements; corpus or metric non-reproducibility; new authority domains; or any merge requiring bypass, force-push, direct-main write or ruleset weakening.

Rollback is non-destructive. Disable derived services, rebuild projections and supersede records/edges through new events while preserving every source, warning, decision, PR, commit and receipt.
