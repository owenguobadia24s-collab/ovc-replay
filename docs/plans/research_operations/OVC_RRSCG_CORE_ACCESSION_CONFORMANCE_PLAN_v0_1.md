## 1. Objective

Create the smallest repository-native scientific capability needed to replay the durable RRSCG machinery over long governed populations, later separately-authorised asset classes and later separately-authorised clocks, while leaving specialised research portable through ResearchProtocol/IROF adapters.

The core is deliberately limited to:

1. **R2 continuation-constraint kernel** — representation-robust consensus/support/ambiguity/abstention surface;
2. **D9 observer-state faculty** — constraint state, geometry and kinematics over the lawful R2 constraint surface;
3. **D10 reducer** — narrow C-LAST reducer successor at the reducer layer only;
4. **IROF adapter** — typed StageSpec transport into the existing PopulationSpec/PipelineProfile/AuthorityBindings execution plane;
5. **reference/parity fixtures and claim caps** — historical mechanical equivalence and negative scientific doctrine.

This plan does not import the RRSCG specialist programme tree, memory/metastability studies, regions, drivers, pretransition, boundaries, SFF, Representation Robustness, the named Observer-State Geometry nomination, or AOEC wholesale.

## 6. Multiclock boundary

Generic multiclock coordinate/alignment/correspondence is **not** part of the P1 RRSCG core accession packet. It begins only after single-clock RRSCG parity and capacity are proven.

Forward doctrine is already constrained by LSIAC-R2:

- clock covariance does not imply a shared categorical phase lattice;
- temporal nesting/morphology resemblance does not establish stable compositional hierarchy;
- `TV120_NATIVE` is not silently equal to canonical `2H_A_L`;
- a failed common geometry carrier is not proof of no multiscale information.
