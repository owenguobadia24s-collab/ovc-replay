# C2P2-PS0 ObjectPack Candidate Discrimination Report v0.1

Status: PRESELECTION RESEARCH COMPLETE / NO CANDIDATE SELECTED

## Constitutional finding

C2P and C2E remain parallel continuity owners. C2P owns persistent referential identity; C2E owns causal episode identity/lifecycle. C2E evidence may be consumed by C2P only when an ObjectPack explicitly declares it, and episode identity is never sufficient to establish object identity. Family and downstream semantic evidence remain forbidden identity sources.

## Candidate A — STRICT CONTINUITY

`C2P2-PS0-OP-A-STRICT-CONTINUITY-v1`

Purpose: high-specificity control. Requires exact geometry-signature continuity in addition to hard scope and chronology. It is expected to minimise accidental identity carryover but to fragment objects whenever geometry evolves lawfully.

Primary falsifier: excessive new-assertion/recurrence creation under small but owner-lawful structural evolution.

Research role: strict lower-bound control for persistence claims.

## Candidate B — RELATIONAL CONTINUITY

`C2P2-PS0-OP-B-RELATIONAL-CONTINUITY-v1`

Purpose: allow geometry evolution when structural role, registered relation topology, owner-declared geometry compatibility, chronology and ambiguity controls remain satisfied.

Primary falsifier: inappropriate continuity through a structurally material geometry/topology change that the exact predicates admit.

Research role: principal C2-only persistent-identity hypothesis.

## Candidate C — EPISODE-ENRICHED CONTINUITY

`C2P2-PS0-OP-C-EPISODE-ENRICHED-CONTINUITY-v1`

Purpose: test whether optional declared C2E lineage/boundary evidence improves object-continuity decisions without making C2E mandatory for base C2-only object roles. For explicit episode-relative object roles, the exact ObjectPack may require C2E compatibility; otherwise the C2-only base route remains lawful.

Primary falsifiers: C2E becomes de-facto mandatory for base identity; episode identity alone creates C2P identity; or C2E segmentation differences create unstable object identity without independent structural justification.

Research role: additive challenger measuring the marginal value/cost of C2E evidence while preserving parallel-domain ownership.

## Synthetic/adversarial discrimination

The common PS0 fixture corpus establishes expected disagreement surfaces rather than empirical superiority. All candidates preserve censoring, retirement non-resurrection, ambiguity, split/merge conservation, cross-scale separation, family firewall and downstream-semantic firewall.

Material disagreements are intentional:

- lawful geometry evolution distinguishes strict Candidate A from B/C;
- Candidate C reacts to C2E lineage only where a declared episode-relative dependency exists;
- Candidate A provides a high-specificity fragmentation control;
- Candidate B provides the cleanest C2-only relational-continuity hypothesis;
- Candidate C measures whether optional C2E evidence adds useful discrimination or only couples persistent identity to segmentation.

No synthetic result is sufficient to select a winner or promote a threshold.

## Recommendation for the operator gate

Recommend `PASS` at `C2P2-PS0-GSELECT` **for all three exact candidates to proceed as a preregistered comparative set into C2P2-RS0 preparation**, not as active or preferred ObjectPacks.

Rationale:

1. There is no real-source C2P evidence yet, so eliminating a candidate on synthetic fit alone would be scientifically premature.
2. A/B/C form a useful controlled ladder: exact-signature strictness -> relational C2 continuity -> relational continuity with optional declared C2E evidence.
3. Carrying all three into one common, blinded, source-bound RS0 population allows the system to measure fragmentation, ambiguity, persistence, disagreement and C2E marginal information under identical cutoffs.
4. This recommendation preserves the user's intended C2P/C2E parallelism because Candidate C was corrected to optional-by-default C2E consumption rather than serial dependency.

A GSELECT PASS does not authorize C2P2-RS0 real-source execution. RS0 still requires its own exact operator run authority token, source/population binding, resource envelope, restart/capacity plan and Validation lock.
