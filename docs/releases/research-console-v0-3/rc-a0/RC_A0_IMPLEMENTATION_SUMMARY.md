Status: `COMPLETE_RC_A1_REVIEW_READY`

## Result

RC-A0 replaces the OVC Research Console v0.2 implementation direction with the v0.3 unified-workspaces architecture.

The active top-level structure is frozen as:

- `Overview`
- `Research`
- `System`

The fourteen v0.2 routes are preserved through an explicit migration registry rather than retained as competing primary navigation. Research Desk, Replay, Evidence, Sessions and Queue become one context-preserving Research workspace. Health becomes both an ambient global projection and a detailed System section. Audit becomes a persistent activity stream with full detail retained in System.

- ambient multidimensional health;
- source-derived activity stream;
