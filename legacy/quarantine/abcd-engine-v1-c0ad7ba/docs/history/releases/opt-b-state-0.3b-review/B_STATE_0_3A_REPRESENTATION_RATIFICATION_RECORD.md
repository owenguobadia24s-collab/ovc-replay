## Ratified

1. Acceptance is not a persistent categorical market state.
2. `ACCEPTED_ABOVE`, `ACCEPTED_BELOW` and `ACCEPTED_CORRIDOR` are prohibited as persistent state labels.
3. Level-specific accepted relations remain an observable, non-governing inventory.
4. Every relation remains auditable; a compact boundary view may project the inventory but may not delete, rank or silently select a level.
5. Acceptance cannot suppress displacement, compression, interaction or quality evidence.
6. Freshness remains an exact timestamp or observed-bar age; no qualitative freshness bucket or hidden TTL is authorized.
7. Same-label and opposite-direction relations at different prices may coexist. Only same-level contradiction, classifier ambiguity or invalid frontier bounds constitute conflict.

## Explicitly not ratified

- Promotion of every raw acceptance confirmation to the primary state timeline.
- A specific acceptance-event frequency or threshold.
- A default human-facing inventory projection.
- Any elapsed-time retirement, relevance score or hidden best-level selector.
- OPT-C outcomes, edge, recommendation, production or execution authority.

The one-bar raw acceptance event in `B-STATE-0.3a` remains admissible audit evidence while its state-timeline role undergoes semantic review.
